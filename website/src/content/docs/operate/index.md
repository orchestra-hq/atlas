Day-to-day operation of an Atlas endpoint or fleet, from the terminal.

- **[API keys](/atlas/operate/api-keys/)** — mint, scope, and revoke client keys.
- **[Usage metering](/atlas/operate/usage/)** — the token ledger by key, model, and worker.
- **[Observability](/atlas/operate/observability/)** — Prometheus `/metrics`, `atlas status`, and
  `atlas top`.
- **[TLS](/atlas/operate/tls/)** — ACME, an operator cert, or self-signed + pin.
- **[Scaling & operations](/atlas/operate/scaling/)** — deploy/scale/stop, drain, spot, and weights.
